    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is KinetraX and how does it work?

    KinetraX is a marketplace that connects coaches and athletes through a Telegram Mini App built on TON blockchain. Coaches create training sessions on an interactive map, athletes discover and book them, and smart contracts handle the payment — automatically splitting funds between the coach and the platform with minimal fees (0–3%).

    How do blockchain payments work?

    When you book a session, your payment goes to a smart contract on the TON blockchain. The contract acts as an escrow — holding funds until the session is complete, then automatically releasing payment to the coach. This eliminates the need for a trusted third party and ensures both sides are protected.

    How does trainer verification work?

    Trainers submit their credentials and certifications through the platform. Our team reviews the documents and records the verification on the blockchain. Verified trainers receive a badge visible to all users, building trust and credibility.

    What is the KTX token and why should I care?

    KTX is the native utility token of KinetraX built on the TON Jetton standard. When coaches accept KTX as payment, they pay zero platform fees — keeping 100% of their earnings. Users benefit from payment discounts, campaign incentives, and priority access to selected platform features. The total supply is capped at 21 million tokens.

    What are the platform fees?

    KinetraX charges 0–3% on sessions paid in TON (default is 2%). Sessions paid in KTX tokens have zero platform fees — the coach keeps everything. This is dramatically lower than traditional platforms which charge 15–30%.

    Is my data and money secure?

    All transactions are secured by smart contracts on the TON blockchain — immutable and transparent. Personal data is encrypted and stored off-chain. We never have access to your wallet or private keys. Authentication uses your Telegram account, so there are no passwords to leak.
