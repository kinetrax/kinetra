Frequently Asked Questions

How do blockchain payments work?

Blockchain payments on KinetraX use smart contracts on the TON blockchain. When you book a session, funds are held in escrow and automatically released to the trainer after completion. This ensures security and transparency for both parties.

How does trainer verification work?

Trainers submit their credentials and certifications through our platform. Our verification team reviews the documents and verifies them on the blockchain. Once verified, trainers receive a blockchain-verified badge that builds trust with athletes.

What are token staking and rewards?

Staking KinetraX tokens allows you to lock your tokens for a period and earn rewards. The longer you stake, the higher the rewards. Stakers also receive discounts on platform fees and exclusive access to premium features.

Is my data and money secure?

Yes! All transactions are secured by smart contracts on the TON blockchain, which is immutable and transparent. Your personal data is encrypted and stored securely. We never have access to your wallet or private keys.

What are the platform fees?

KinetraX charges only 2-5% in platform fees, significantly lower than traditional booking platforms. If you pay with KinetraX tokens, you receive additional discounts. Stakers enjoy even lower fees.
