# KinetraX — agent context (marketing repo)

## Product

KinetraX is a **Telegram Mini App** marketplace on **TON** connecting **coaches** and **athletes** for paid training sessions: map discovery, booking, TON/KNT payments, smart-contract splits, verification codes.

## AI positioning

- **Purpose**: **Athletes** — find the **right sessions** faster (ranking, explanations, optional natural-language search). **Coaches** — clearer listings (titles/tags/descriptions) and **better-matched visibility** to intent-aligned athletes. Same AI layer, role-aware UX in the Mini App.
- **Boundaries**: AI assists **discovery and copy**; **trust** still comes from verification, reviews, and **on-chain** payment rules. Do not imply AI replaces compliance, medical advice, or escrow.
- **Voice**: Confident and specific; avoid hype. Prefer “AI-assisted matching”, “ranked for your goals”, “suggested sessions” over vague “revolutionary AI”.

## Repo role

This repository is the **static marketing site** (`index.html`, `whitepaper.html`, legal pages, `css/styles.css`, `js/translations.js`). The **Mini App** is a separate codebase; when documenting AI, keep claims aligned with shipped behavior.
